Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to convert a video to 3D? +
Our AI processes standard product videos in an average of 2.5 minutes. Once generated, your 3D GLTF and USDZ models are immediately ready to preview and embed.
Do shoppers need to download an AR app? +
No! JoshStream Web uses native WebAR (iOS QuickLook & Android SceneViewer). Shoppers simply click a button or scan a QR code to view the product in their space inside Safari or Chrome.
What camera equipment do I need? +
Any modern smartphone (iPhone 12+ or Android equivalent) works perfectly. Just slowly walk around the product in good lighting or use a $15 motorized turntable.
Does JoshStream Web slow down my store page load? +
Not at all. Our WebGL viewer script is asynchronously loaded (under 25KB gzipped) and models are streamed via globally edge-cached CDNs.
